# Welcome to the garden

This is a little corner of the internet for **learning out loud**. It is not a course, a portfolio, or a checklist to race through. It is a place to notice what I am curious about and leave a trail I can follow later.

## What belongs here?

- Notes from books, projects, and conversations
- Questions that do not have answers yet
- Useful ideas that deserve more than a browser bookmark
- Small experiments and the things they teach me

> A learning garden grows through attention, not urgency.

## How to use this space

Each card opens a Markdown note. The cards, categories, and summaries come from `manifest.json`; the writing itself lives in this folder. That separation keeps the site simple and makes each note easy to edit anywhere.

Start with a rough thought. It can become clearer as it grows.
